Privacy Policy for Baza AI

Application Name: Baza AI • Developer: BazaDevSpace • Effective Date: September 11, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which BazaDevSpace ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of the Baza AI personal assistant operating via the Meta WhatsApp Cloud platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Baza AI on WhatsApp, we process:

  • Account Identifiers: Your WhatsApp phone number (sender ID) and profile display name.
  • Conversation History & Media: Text messages, conversation turns, and voice note recordings you transmit to the assistant.
  • Proactive Long-Term Memory: Personal preferences, facts, and habits you explicitly share that Baza AI autonomously stores to personalize future assistance.
  • Task and Reminder Data: To-do lists, completion states, scheduled reminder times, and recurrence frequencies.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Provide intelligent conversational replies and multimodal audio processing.
  • Maintain persistent memory across conversations so you do not have to repeat context.
  • Dispatch scheduled reminder notifications to your WhatsApp account at the times you request.
  • Maintain security, verify Meta webhook HMAC-SHA256 signatures, and prevent duplicate message processing.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We utilize trusted infrastructure partners under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp Cloud API): Ingress and egress messaging transmission.
  • Google Cloud (Gemini API): Natural language understanding and tool invocation. Conversations are never used to train public Google AI models.
  • Supabase, Inc.: Encrypted cloud database storage for user profiles, memories, and schedules.

5. We Never Sell Your Data

Zero Sale Policy: We do not sell, trade, rent, or lease your personal information, phone numbers, or conversation content to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers.

6. User Data Deletion Instructions (Meta Compliance)

How to Request Full Data Deletion:

In accordance with Meta Platform Policies, you may delete all data stored about you at any time:

  1. In-Chat Deletion: Send Delete my data or Reset all my memories directly to Baza AI on WhatsApp. Our service will immediately purge your stored memory notes and conversation turn history.
  2. Email Request: Send an email to privacy@bazadevspace.com or info@bazadevspace.com with your WhatsApp phone number and subject line "Data Deletion Request". Your records will be permanently deleted within 48 business hours.

7. Data Security & Retention

All data transmissions are encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS). Webhook deliveries are authenticated with SHA-256 HMAC cryptographic signatures. Data is retained only while you actively use the assistant or until you request its deletion.
